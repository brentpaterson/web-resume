Technical Projects & Solutions

Architectural case studies in enterprise automation, billing, and distributed systems.

Automated Consumption & Dynamic Billing Pipeline

Docker
Technologies: Python, SQL, REST APIs, NetSuite, Salesforce

Problem: Cloud infrastructure usage data was fragmented across multiple telemetry clusters, making timely billing calculations and overage warnings difficult for account teams.

Solution: Architected an automated data pipeline that aggregates usage consumption, normalizes metrics against contractual entitlement tiers, and triggers real-time alerts to the sales organization. Automated the downstream creation of complex NetSuite sales orders and billing schedules.

Impact: Eliminated end-of-quarter billing delays and provided account executives with early signals for expansion opportunities.

AI-Powered Unstructured Document Processing

Docker
Technologies: Python, Machine Learning APIs, OCR, Webhooks

Problem: Operations and revenue teams were spending hundreds of hours manually keying dynamic, variable data from supplier agreements, partner invoices, and order forms.

Solution: Deployed an AI document processing ingestion engine that validates incoming documents, performs schema normalization, extracts tabular items, and posts verified records directly into core systems.

Impact: Drastically cut manual data entry errors and reduced operational document turnaround from days to minutes.

Secure On-Premises Integration Gateway

ThoughtSpot
Technologies: Java, Node.js, Linux, SSL/TLS, Custom API Middleware

Problem: High-security customer environments prohibited direct cloud connections to internal database clusters.

Solution: Designed and configured a dedicated on-premises server runtime acting as an encrypted proxy gateway, ensuring only authenticated and whitelisted ThoughtSpot transactions were processed through internal firewalls.

Impact: Enabled enterprise customers in banking and healthcare to connect internal analytics while adhering strictly to compliance mandates.

Event-Driven Salesforce-to-Slack Communication Hub

Cloudera
Technologies: JavaScript, Salesforce Outbound Messaging, Slack Webhooks API

Problem: Fast-moving sales and customer support teams lacked immediate cross-functional visibility into key customer milestone transitions.

Solution: Created a bi-directional webhook service listening for Salesforce contract updates and dispatching contextual, interactive Slack notification cards with deep links.

Impact: Improved team response times and kept cross-functional partners aligned in real-time.
